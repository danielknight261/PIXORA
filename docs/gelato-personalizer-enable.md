# Enable Gelato Personalizer on Snapp Daddy

Store: https://snappdaddy.com

## Turn Personalizer ON (Shopify Admin)

1. Open: https://snappdaddy.com/admin
2. Go to **Online Store → Themes**
3. On your **current theme**, click **Customize**
4. In the left sidebar (bottom area), open **App embeds** (puzzle-piece / embeds icon — not “Sections”)
5. Find **Gelato Personalizer** (or “Gelato Personalization”)
6. Toggle it **ON**
7. Click **Save** (top right)

If you don’t see Gelato Personalizer in App embeds:

- Confirm the Gelato app is installed: **Settings → Apps and sales channels**
- Open the **Gelato** app once so it finishes connecting
- Refresh the theme editor and check App embeds again
- Some Gelato personalization features need **Gelato+** — check in your Gelato dashboard

## After Personalizer is on

Publish a personalized product from the Gelato app (canvas or mug with an image layer), then open the product page and look for **Personalize**.

Official guide: https://support.gelato.com/en/articles/8996330-how-to-set-up-gelato-s-personalization-studio-on-shopify
