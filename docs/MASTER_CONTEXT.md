# SnapzDaddy

Tagline:
Turn Moments Into Keepsakes

## Vision

SnapzDaddy is a modern photo personalization platform inspired by Snapfish, Photobox and Moonpig.

Users can:

- Create accounts
- Upload photos
- Personalise products
- Preview products live
- Save designs
- Purchase products
- Track orders

All products are fulfilled by third-party print-on-demand providers.

SnapzDaddy owns:

- Customer relationship
- Frontend
- Checkout
- User accounts

SnapzDaddy does not own:

- Printing equipment
- Warehouses
- Inventory
